The self-driving car.

2022 — 2025

An autonomous RC car that brings cameras, distance sensing, and on-board computing together to understand its surroundings and decide what to do next.

hardware / front-viewEXPLORER
01 / 04
The hardware. Tap a marker to look a little closer.

THE IDEA

More than one way to see.

Cameras give the vehicle visual context. LiDAR adds distance measurements. The Raspberry Pi brings those inputs together so the software can respond to nearby obstacles.

THE INSPIRATION

Built for imperfect roads.

Growing up around Massachusetts roads made me curious about detecting potholes and surface damage. I explored low-mounted sensing as part of the larger perception system.
