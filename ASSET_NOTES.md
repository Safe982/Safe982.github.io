## Artwork preparation

Built-in ImageGen edit mode was used to prepare the two supplied pixel illustrations with alpha transparency. The original uploads were preserved. The prepared project assets are `assets/ayan.png` and `assets/campus.png`.

Remove only the white background and output genuine alpha transparency. Preserve the exact character identity, all pixel art, colors, pose, laptop, books, pencil cup, and the baseline. No additions, no text, no checkerboard pixels. Crop framing to the entire artwork with small transparent margins, leaving no large empty top area. This is an asset for his dark personal website.
